# Voice

## Canonical source

The full voice guide lives at `design-system/README.md` under "Content
fundamentals." Read that when writing or reviewing copy. This file is
a short summary plus repo-specific guidance.

## Core rules

- **First-person singular** ("I build," "I worked"). Never "we." Never
  "Trevor" in third person on the site itself.
- **Sentence case** for titles, headings, and buttons: "Book a 15-min
  intro," "What I do," "How an engagement works."
- **UPPERCASE for mono eyebrows only:** `WHAT I DO`, `BACKGROUND`,
  `01 — BUILD`.
- **Specifics over claims.** "Ten years in React" beats "deep React
  experience." "25 recommendations on LinkedIn" beats "well-regarded
  by past colleagues."
- **No emoji.** Anywhere. Ever.
- **No exclamation marks** in marketing copy. The closing dot does the
  work.
- **No hype superlatives** ("world-class," "best-in-class," "10x,"
  "transform"). If a sentence sounds like a startup deck, rewrite it.

## Sentence shapes that work

Lifted from the live site copy as reference:

> Senior fullstack engineer. *Ships the thing.*

> I build production web apps for founders and product teams who need a
> senior engineer who can scope, ship, and explain the tradeoffs.

> Four kinds of project, scoped tight, shipped fast.

> Almost all of my recent work is under NDA. The engagements below are
> anonymized but specific. Happy to walk through particulars on a call.

> 15 minutes. No pitch. Either I'm a fit or I can recommend someone
> who is.

The italic crimson display word on a Fraunces headline (e.g. *Ships the
thing.*) is the brand's signature type move; keep it scarce.

## Banned words and phrases

From `design-system/README.md` — if any appears in a draft, the
sentence is probably wrong, not just the word:

- passionate, passionate about
- leverage (verb), utilize
- scalable (unless followed by a specific number)
- user-centric, customer-centric
- innovative, cutting-edge, bleeding edge
- synergy, synergize
- rockstar, ninja, wizard, guru
- journey, excited to, thrilled to
- powered by AI, AI-powered
- transform, unlock, elevate
- 10x, world-class, best-in-class
- passion project, deep dive (as noun)

## Reassurance lines

A recurring shape on the site: small mute-gray captions under a CTA
that reduce friction. Keep them honest and specific.

- "Typical response within one business day. Free consult, no pitch."
- "15 minutes. No pitch. Either I'm a fit or I can recommend someone
  who is."

## Placeholders in code

When real content isn't available yet (engagement rows, future testimonial
slots), use bracketed instruction prose, not lorem ipsum:

```
[ Replace with one specific sentence — what was the problem,
  what did you build, what changed. ]
```

This is the canonical placeholder pattern in the design system. It
makes the gap visible and instructs the next reader how to close it.

## Voice review checklist

Before any copy ships:

- First person?
- Sentence case for titles?
- Specific (numbers, names, real decisions) over claims?
- Free of banned words?
- Would I read this if it weren't mine?
- Does it sound like Trevor talking, or like a portfolio site?

The last question is the most important.
